# Copy rules (screen, voiceover, captions)

## Facts

- **Never invent** quotes, testimonials, titles, numbers or speaker facts.
- Every fact on screen needs a source: the request, the person themselves, an official
  page, or reputable press. Check recent or large claims (valuations, funding, headcount)
  with a web search before using them; AI search summaries are a lead, not a source.
- Single-source or third-party-only facts: use them only if the user agrees, and list
  them as "unverified" in the delivery note.
- Facts change: a person's current role, a company's offering, a price, a deadline.
  Prefer what the person says about themselves. (A speaker once asked us to drop a
  programme that is now B2B only and name their current company instead.)
- No deadlines, prices or "applications open" on screen unless the request confirms them
  as current. Old takes often mention expired dates; cut those lines.
- Quotes on screen are what the person actually said in the footage, translated
  faithfully if needed. Mark nothing as a quote that was not said.

## People

- Names exactly as the person writes them (see `pronunciation.md`).
- Use only roles you can source. "CEO · weXelerate" yes; a guessed job title no.
- Pronouns: use the person's own if known; otherwise write around them (name, role,
  "they"). Never guess from a name or photo.

## Style

- No em dashes. Use a full stop, comma or colon.
- Short declarative lines. Numbers over adjectives.
- weXelerate: sentence case, no emoji anywhere.
- Future Forward: load the `ff-copy` skill; never "This is not a conference for spectators".
- Recaps inform, they do not sell. A CTA only when the request has one.

## Captions (the post text)

Deliver one ready-to-paste caption per platform asked for:

- **Instagram**: first line is a hook with a number; 3 short blocks (one per hero); a
  question to drive comments; "save this" if it is educational; 4–6 hashtags.
- **LinkedIn**: same structure, a bit more context per hero, tag the people and companies
  (list their handles if you know them, otherwise remind the user to tag).
- weXelerate posts: no emoji. Future Forward posts: emoji sparing, `#FutureForward2026 #FF26`.
- Hashtags: brand tag first (`#weXelerate` or `#FF26`), then topic tags. No hashtag walls.
